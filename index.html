About Me

AI and data engineer with over three years of experience in large-scale model inference, distributed systems, data engineering, and cloud infrastructure. At Huawei, I contributed to vLLM-Omni, developing and optimizing multimodal inference pipelines for models including Qwen3-Omni, MiniCPM-o 4.5, AURA, VoxCPM, and Qwen3-TTS. My work improved latency and throughput through asynchronous chunking, continuous batching, parallelism, KV-cache optimization, and prefill-decoding disaggregation. Previously, at HKT, I built and maintained ETL pipelines using AWS, Spark, Informatica, DataStage, Oracle, Redshift, and Hadoop, while supporting CI/CD and data workflow automation. I also gained full-stack and DevOps experience at the Hong Kong Hospital Authority, working with React, Jenkins, Docker, OpenShift, and GitHub Actions. I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Data Science at UC San Diego and am interested in AI infrastructure, LLM inference acceleration, machine learning systems, and data engineering.
